Worthwell Estate sales, gently guided

Start with a photo. See what a sale could bring.

Worthwell helps families and solo operators run a first estate sale or liquidation. Photograph a room, review starting prices, and get a plan you can hold — at your own pace.

How it works

Four calm steps, starting with a picture.

You can begin without creating an account. An account is only needed when you want to save your sale and pick it up on another device.

1

Photograph a room

Start with a room photo to see furniture and smaller things together, or snap a single piece you’re unsure about.

2

Review each price

You’ll get a fair-value range and a starting price, labeled as an AI estimate until you tap “Looks right” or edit it.

3

Shape the sale

When does everything need to be gone? Will you sell in person, online, or both? Where is the home? You’ll get a simple plan, such as “Your sale in 3 weekends.”

4

Keep it in one place

An item board tracks every piece, with a projected total — and money actually sold, kept separate. Export a spreadsheet whenever you need one.

What’s in the app

Clear prices, and you stay in control.

These are features in the live app today. Anything still being built is marked Coming soon.

A room or a single item

Photograph a whole room, or one object. You can add more photos, name the room, and keep going when you’re ready.

You approve every estimate

Every AI price stays an estimate until you accept or change it. Edit the name, condition, value, or starting price whenever you need to.

A running total, honestly split

See a projected low, expected, and high for the sale, with money already sold shown on its own — not mixed into the forecast.

A timeline that shapes prices

Choose how soon you’re hoping to sell. If you change that timeline later, Worthwell asks before it recalculates unapproved starting prices.

List on its own, bundle, or Donate

Each item gets a plain recommendation. If two things look like a set, the app can suggest pricing them together — and you can skip it.

Comparable listings, only if they’re real

For higher-value pieces, Worthwell looks for comparable listings and keeps only the ones it actually opened. If it can’t find reliable ones, it says so.

Your photos stay put until you sign up

Photos remain on the device until you create an account. Sign in with Google or email to continue on your phone and computer.

Automatic markdowns Coming soon

A timeline-based schedule that lowers prices as the last sale day approaches, with a chance to see, skip, or undo each drop.

Donation pickup suggestions Coming soon

A nudge to schedule a pickup when something is large, low-value, and not moving — or already marked Donate.

A Settings page you control Coming soon

A home for pricing preferences and listing rules, so your choices stay saved instead of living in a conversation.

Who it’s for

Built for a first sale, not a warehouse crew.

Families

If you’re sorting a home’s things for the first time, Worthwell gives you prices and a plan without handing the sale — and a large share of it — to a company.

Solo operators

If you’re running a liquidation yourself, you get a single place to catalog rooms, approve prices, and see what the sale is adding up to.

The Hudson Valley, first

The local starting place is the Hudson Valley in New York. If you’re elsewhere and want to try the private beta, you’re still welcome to join the list.

A quieter pace

If you’re doing this after a loss, the app is meant to stay out of the way.

Grief makes it harder to concentrate, so Worthwell keeps to one clear step at a time, with a visible next action, and nothing you have to remember from the screen before. The work is the sale — not a reminder of why you’re here.

Questions

A few things people ask.

What is the private beta?

A small, early group using Worthwell and telling us what’s missing. Joining the list doesn’t create an account or guarantee a spot; we’ll follow up when there’s room. The live app is already available to try if you’d like to look around first.

Is this only for the Hudson Valley?

That’s the first place we’re focused, because it’s where Worthwell was shaped. If you’re somewhere else and facing a first estate sale, you’re still welcome to join the list.

Do I need an account to try it?

No. You can start with a photo. Photos stay on your device until you sign up. Create a free account when you want to save the sale and open it on another phone or computer. Guest use has daily limits so the service stays available.

How are prices calculated?

A starting list price begins with a fair market value, then is adjusted for condition and pricing tier and rounded. The result is shown as an AI estimate until you approve or edit it. For higher-value items, the app looks for comparable listings and keeps only ones it actually opened. You can always change a price.

Does it cost anything?

Joining the waitlist is free, and you can try the live app without paying. Free use has some limits. A paid plan for whole-home sales is in the works; we’ll share any pricing before you’re asked to pay.
